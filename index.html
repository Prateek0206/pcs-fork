Welcome to PCS KALAHANDI


Pragati Concept School, Kalahandi, is a co-educational English-medium institution that adheres to the CBSE curriculum.

We offer a vibrant, verdant, and secure environment where the joy of learning is cultivated in every pupil.

The hallmark of PRAGATI institutions lies in our unwavering commitment to quality education, academic excellence, and a holistic approach to development — all guided by a dedicated and compassionate faculty.
